Caring For Nathanael

Health Protocols & Safety Measures

To ensure a safe, healthy, and joyous gathering for baby Nathanael and all our beloved guests, please keep these thoughtful precautions in mind.

No Kissing Baby

Baby Nathanael's immune system is still developing. We kindly ask everyone to refrain from kissing his cheeks or hands.

Sanitize Your Hands

Please sanitize your hands before holding or interacting with Nathanael.

No Smoking / Vaping

Both ceremony and reception venues are strictly smoke-free and vape-free to keep the air clean for baby Nathanael.

Rest If Feeling Unwell

If you are experiencing fever, cough, or flu-like symptoms, please send your warm love from home. We fully understand!

Dress Code & Palette

We invite our loved guests to dress in light, serene hot air balloon pastel colors.

Special Request for Godparents

Godparents (Ninongs & Ninangs) are kindly requested to wear WHITE attire.

Your white attire symbolizes purity and spiritual guidance for baby Nathanael as you stand by him during the Holy Sacrament.
